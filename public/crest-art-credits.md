# Crest art credits

All crest emblems are original project drawings. The 31 emblems offered in the crest editor are filled silhouettes on the 24-unit emblem grid (`crest-emblems.js`); the retired emblems that older saved crests may still wear are drawn inline in `crest.js`.

Earlier versions of the Dragon, Wyvern, Hydra, Twin hydra, Minotaur, Ouroboros (Lorc), Pegasus (Skoll), Unicorn, Spartan, Helm, Serpent (Delapouite), and Medusa (Cathelineau) adapted icons from [Game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net) under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Those drawings were replaced and no longer ship.

# Crest art credits

Filled Dragon, Wyvern, Hydra, and Twin hydra silhouettes adapted from **Lorc** via [Game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net), under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

Original SVG sources:

- [Dragon head — Lorc](https://github.com/game-icons/icons/blob/master/lorc/dragon-head.svg)
- [Wyvern — Lorc](https://github.com/game-icons/icons/blob/master/lorc/wyvern.svg)
- [Hydra — Lorc](https://github.com/game-icons/icons/blob/master/lorc/hydra.svg)
- [Double dragon — Lorc](https://github.com/game-icons/icons/blob/master/lorc/double-dragon.svg)

Adaptations remove the background, take the crest's ink color, and scale the original silhouettes into Empire's emblem grid. Other crest drawings are original project SVGs.

Phoenix is an original SVG. Lion and War horse are vector contours derived from user-supplied references; only the animal figures are used.

Additional filled crest silhouettes: Pegasus by Skoll; Minotaur by Lorc; Unicorn by Delapouite. Source: https://github.com/game-icons/icons (skoll/pegasus.svg, lorc/minotaur.svg, delapouite/unicorn.svg). Licensed CC BY 3.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/. Adaptations remove the background, take the crest's ink color, and scale to the 24-unit emblem grid. Double-headed eagle is original project-native SVG with symmetric curved wings, paired hooked-beak heads, talons, and fan tail.

Medusa adapts [Medusa head by Cathelineau](https://github.com/game-icons/icons/blob/master/cathelineau/medusa-head.svg), [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Background removed; ink color applied; scaled to the crest grid.

Spartan and Helm adapt Delapouite’s [Spartan helmet](https://github.com/game-icons/icons/blob/master/delapouite/spartan-helmet.svg) and [Centurion helmet](https://github.com/game-icons/icons/blob/master/delapouite/centurion-helmet.svg), CC BY 3.0. Backgrounds removed; ink color applied; scaled to the crest grid.

Serpent adapts Cobra by Delapouite, CC BY 3.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/. Source: https://github.com/game-icons/icons/blob/master/delapouite/cobra.svg . Adaptations remove the background, take the crest's ink color, and scale to the 24-unit grid. Wolf is original project-native filled SVG with curved mane and cheek cutouts.

Ouroboros adapts [Lorc’s Ouroboros](https://github.com/game-icons/icons/blob/master/lorc/ouroboros.svg), [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Background removed; ink color applied; scaled to the crest grid.

Roman eagle (aquila) is original project-native filled SVG inspired by historical Roman standard motifs: a profile eagle with spread wings, lightning bolts, and laurel. No source artwork or lettering is embedded.

The Griffin is a vector contour derived from a user-supplied reference: the traced figure was reweighted, given a newly drawn wing, and traced again. The Eagle (head erased), Gladius in laurel, Spears in saltire, radiate Crown, Sun in splendor, Cross pattée, Botonny cross, and Maltese cross are original project drawings in the same style (`crest-heraldic.js`).

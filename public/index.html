    How to play

    GoalBe the leader of the last empire standing.

    Setup

    1. Create a room, or leave the name blank for a random one.
    2. Tap Share and send the link, or have friends enter the room code at empire.ihtfy.com.
    3. Enter your real name and a secret name.
    4. Short on players? Add bots from the room menu.
    5. Press Reveal the names once everyone is in.

    Play

    1. Pick a secret name you hope nobody links to you.
    2. Memorize. Names are read twice in random order. No recording. Leaders can vote to hear them again.
    3. Take turns clockwise. Only leaders take turns.
    4. Guess. Name another leader and their secret name.
      • Wrong Your turn ends.
      • Right They join your empire and you guess again.
    5. Win as the last leader whose name is never guessed.

    Good to know

    • A conquered leader brings their whole empire along.
    • Members help guess and protect their leader's name.
    • Members may talk, but only leaders make official guesses.
